Most businesses don’t need more software.

Maali Wassan

I build automation systems for companies that have outgrown doing things by hand. Most of it runs on n8n, custom APIs and AI agents: systems that answer inquiries, keep platforms in sync, qualify leads and keep work moving after everyone has logged off.

Over the last 6+ years I’ve worked with teams in media, healthcare, automotive, industrial supply and high-volume operations, sometimes directly with leadership and sometimes behind an agency. Either way, the aim is the same: automation that keeps working as the business grows.
